<h1 class="text-3xl font-bold text-gray-900 mb-6">Understanding Walkingpad Motor Power</h1></p><p class="text-gray-700 leading-relaxed mb-4">The Walkingpad motor is one of the most important components affecting durability and performance.</p><p class="text-gray-700 leading-relaxed mb-4"><h2 class="text-2xl font-bold text-gray-900 mb-4 mt-8">Types of Motors</h2></p><p class="text-gray-700 leading-relaxed mb-4">Most Walkingpad use DC motors, while some commercial ones use AC motors.</p><p class="text-gray-700 leading-relaxed mb-4"><strong class="font-semibold">Comparison:</strong><ul class="list-disc font-semibold list-inside mb-4 space-y-1">
<li class="mb-2"><strong class="font-semibold">DC Motors</strong>: Quieter, better for home use</li>
</ul><li class="mb-2"><strong class="font-semibold">AC Motors</strong>: More powerful, suited for gyms</li></p><p class="text-gray-700 leading-relaxed mb-4"><h2 class="text-2xl font-bold text-gray-900 mb-4 mt-8">Horsepower (HP)</h2></p><p class="text-gray-700 leading-relaxed mb-4">There are two values to know:<ul class="list-disc font-semibold list-inside mb-4 space-y-1">
<li class="mb-2"><strong class="font-semibold">Peak HP</strong>: Max output (not sustainable)</li>
</ul><li class="mb-2"><strong class="font-semibold">Continuous HP (CHP)</strong>: Sustained power (more important)</li></p><p class="text-gray-700 leading-relaxed mb-4"><strong class="font-semibold">Recommendation:</strong><ul class="list-disc font-semibold list-inside mb-4 space-y-1">
<li class="mb-2">Walkers: 1.75–2.0 CHP</li>
</ul><li class="mb-2">Joggers: 2.5 CHP</li><ul class="list-disc font-semibold list-inside mb-4 space-y-1">
<li class="mb-2">Runners: 3.0+ CHP</li>
</ul>

A good motor ensures longevity and smooth performance.
