<h1 class="text-3xl font-bold text-gray-900 mb-6">10 Best Treadmill Workouts for Weight Loss in 2025</h1></p><p class="text-gray-700 leading-relaxed mb-4">Treadmill workouts are one of the most effective ways to burn calories and lose weight. High-Intensity Interval Training (HIIT)</h2></p><p class="text-gray-700 leading-relaxed mb-4">HIIT workouts alternate between high-intensity bursts and recovery periods. This method burns more calories in less time and continues to burn calories even after your workout.</p><p class="text-gray-700 leading-relaxed mb-4"><strong class="font-semibold">Workout Structure:</strong><ul class="list-disc font-semibold list-inside mb-4 space-y-1">

<li class="mb-2">Cool-down: 5 minutes at easy pace</li>
</ul>

Incline Walking</h2></p><p class="text-gray-700 leading-relaxed mb-4">Walking on an incline significantly increases calorie burn without the high impact of running.</p><p class="text-gray-700 leading-relaxed mb-4"><strong class="font-semibold">Workout Structure:</strong><ul class="list-disc font-semibold list-inside mb-4 space-y-1">

<li class="mb-2">2 minutes at 60% effort</li>
</ul><li class="mb-2">2 minutes at 70% effort</li><ul class="list-disc font-semibold list-inside mb-4 space-y-1">
<li class="mb-2">2 minutes at 80% effort</li>
</ul><li class="mb-2">2 minutes at 90% effort</li><ul class="list-disc font-semibold list-inside mb-4 space-y-1">
<li class="mb-2">Reverse the pyramid back down</li>
</ul>
<h2 class="text-2xl font-bold text-gray-900 mb-4 mt-8">4. Steady-State Cardio</h2></p><p class="text-gray-700 leading-relaxed mb-4">Maintaining a consistent moderate intensity for extended periods builds endurance and burns fat.</p><p class="text-gray-700 leading-relaxed mb-4"><strong class="font-semibold">Workout Structure:</strong><ul class="list-disc font-semibold list-inside mb-4 space-y-1">
<li class="mb-2">30-60 minutes at 65-75% max heart rate</li>
</ul><li class="mb-2">Focus on sustainable pace</li><ul class="list-disc font-semibold list-inside mb-4 space-y-1">
<li class="mb-2">Perfect for beginners</li>
</ul>
<h2 class="text-2xl font-bold text-gray-900 mb-4 mt-8">5. Sprint Intervals</h2></p><p class="text-gray-700 leading-relaxed mb-4">Short, intense sprints followed by active recovery periods maximize calorie burn.</p><p class="text-gray-700 leading-relaxed mb-4"><strong class="font-semibold">Workout Structure:</strong><ul class="list-disc font-semibold list-inside mb-4 space-y-1">
