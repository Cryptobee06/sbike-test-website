Sportstech sWalk title

Sportstech is a German fitness equipment manufacturer known for innovative home fitness solutions. The sWalk Walkingpad represents their flagship model, combining advanced shock absorption technology with smart connectivity features.

  • ✔Speed up to 20 km/h
  • ✔ 15 gradient levels up to a maximum of 15%

  • ✔rotatable 21.5" touch display
