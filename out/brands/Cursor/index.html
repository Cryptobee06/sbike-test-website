Cursor Walking Pad

The Cursor Walking Pad is a compact, foldable walking treadmill designed for home and office users who prefer light movement throughout the day. It focuses on simplicity, portability, and ease of use, making it suitable for beginners or those who want to stay active while working.

  • ✔Anti-slip belt surface
  • ✔Easy transportation wheels
  • ✔Minimal assembly required
